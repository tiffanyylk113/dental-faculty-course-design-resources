Learning Objectives

Write objectives that communicate the knowledge and skills students should be able to demonstrate.

Competency-based focus: Course-level learning objectives should identify meaningful competencies students are expected to develop, rather than simply listing topics the course will cover. Module objectives may describe smaller knowledge and skill components that build toward those competencies.
What are learning objectives?

Learning objectives describe what students should be able to do after completing a course, module, or learning activity.

Course-level objectives should communicate the broader competencies students are expected to develop. Module-level objectives should describe the smaller knowledge and skills that support those competencies.

How do I write one?
Action verb + knowledge or skill + context

Use observable verbs such as identify, explain, apply, analyze, evaluate, and develop.

Avoid vague verbs such as know, understand, learn, appreciate, or become familiar with.

Compare learning objective examples
Too vague Understand interprofessional collaboration.
Clearer Explain the roles of healthcare professionals in coordinating patient care.
Competency-focused Analyze a patient case to determine when consultation with another healthcare professional is needed and justify the recommended action.
Course-level competency Evaluate research evidence to support clinical decision-making in oral healthcare.
Supporting module objective Interpret the results section of a research article to identify the study's major findings.
Quick Check
  • Does the objective describe what the student will do?
  • Does it identify meaningful knowledge or skill rather than content coverage?
  • Is the action observable and assessable?
  • Does it contribute to a course or professional competency?
  • Is it achievable within the course or module?
